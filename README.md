#ExamClarity

ExamClarity is a web app built for students that turns study notes into summaries, important keywords, definitions and practice questions.It also gives students a study score and random study challenges to make revision more interactive.It was created to help students mainly.Students get overwhelmed, about what to study and how to make notes. Students rely upon YouTube tutors, Brainly and other note‑making sites. These are not useful when students do not have much time. It is easier for students to use and to make notes. It can test students.  It also enhances students studying.ExamClarity turns study notes into summaries, keywords, definitions and practice questions. ExamClarity also gives a study score and random challenges to make revision interactive.

**Features**

⏰ Pomodoro Timer – 25 min focus

🗒️ Sticky Notes – saves points

🎲 Study Challenge – random task to keep students going

🚀 NASA APOD – real space pic daily for motivation

📌 3‑point summary, keywords and quiz
