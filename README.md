ExamClarity
ExamClarity is a student-focused web application that turns study notes into summaries, important keywords, definitions, and practice questions. 
It also gives students a simple study score and random study challenges to make revision more interactive.
I have made this website to help students mainly.
Students get overwhelmed about what to study and how to make notes and they rely upon youtube tutors and Brainly and other note making sites.
But these are not useful when you don't have much time in your hands.
This website is easier for students to use and make notes and can test themselves.Enhancing their studying.

FEATURES

Notes Analyzer >>>It counts words and sentences and finds important terms.

 Automatic Summary >>> This will extract the most relevant sentences from notes.

 Question Generator >>>  It creates Easy, Medium, or Hard practice questions.

 Definition Finder >>>  This identifies sentences that appear to define concepts.

 Study Score >>> It gives a simple score based on the amount and structure of the notes.

 Study Challenge >>>This gives students a random revision activity.

 NASA APOD >>> It adds a daily space image as a fun visual element.

 Limitations
 
This project uses basic text processing  so the generated summaries and questions won't always be perfect.
It works best when the notes are reasonably clear and contain complete sentences.
